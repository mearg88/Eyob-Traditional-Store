THE MEASUREMENT WORKFLOW IS THE PRODUCT
---------------------------------------
Your answer to B2 describes something no normal e-commerce platform does:

  "a user takes measurements and there is a professional who verifies they're
   correct, if not the professional calls the user and takes the measurement by
   phone and edits it in the website"

That is the single most important sentence in this entire document. It means:

  1. A human expert sits between the order and production.
  2. Measurements can be CHANGED BY STAFF after the customer submits them.
  3. A phone call is part of your normal process, not an exception.

This creates a work queue your staff live in every day. It needs to be the
best-designed screen in the entire application, and it needs an audit trail: if
a customer later says "you made it wrong", you must be able to show exactly
